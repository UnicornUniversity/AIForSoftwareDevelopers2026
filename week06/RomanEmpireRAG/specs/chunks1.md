# Text corpora
You have a book in 6 chapters in "week06/data". 

## Your goals

I need you to find optimal parameters for chunking of these chapters for further conversion to RAG.

1. Split text of each chapter into smaller pieces
2. Calculate an average size of such pieces
3. make your guess about optimal chunk size and overlap size
4. Use empty lines, paragraphs and headers as delimiters

## Visualization of results

1. Prepare a standalone HTML page with a result table
2. Take Volume 1, place its text on the HTML page; use background color to show chunks and overlap.
