## 1. Collection Direction

"Open Air" prioritizes breathability, UV protection, and effortless layering. The palette is grounded in off-white, beige, navy, and olive, with pastel accents (pink, light blue) and one bright pop (yellow). Linen and cotton dominate the fabric mix; synthetics appear in technical pieces (DryFlex, UV-Shield, Pocketable Windbreaker).

The season is built around three pillars:

- **Weekday Core** — office-ready neutrals, moderate price band.
- **Weekend Easy** — relaxed cotton and linen, bright accents permitted.
- **Travel Light** — pack-and-go pieces with pocketable or wrinkle-resistant construction.

## 6. Color Pairing Guidance

- **Beige / Off-White ↔ Olive / Navy** — core neutral combinations, works for every scenario.
- **Pink / Light Blue** — pastel accents on tops; keep bottoms neutral.
- **Yellow** — reserve for jackets or caps (UQ-SS26-LJ-001, UQ-SS26-AC-002); avoid on bottoms.
- **Burgundy** — autumn-leaning; use once per outfit for warmth.

---

## 7. Fabric and Weather Guidance

- **Linen pieces** (SH-002, TR-002, TS-003, DR-001, KN-002) wrinkle naturally — do not interpret this as damage.
- **DryFlex and UV-Shield** are the picks for sun exposure, running, and hiking.
- **Pocketable Windbreaker** compresses into its own pouch — ideal for travel and sudden showers; not insulated.
- **Trench Shell Jacket** is water-resistant and transitional; pair with knitwear for cool spring evenings.
