## 1. How to Measure

Body measurements, not garment. Wear light clothing.

- **Bust:** around the fullest part of the bust, tape horizontal.
- **Waist:** around the natural waistline (narrowest point).
- **Hip:** around the fullest part of the hips, approximately 20 cm below the waist.
- **Inseam:** inner leg, crotch to ankle bone.
- **Sleeve:** from the shoulder seam to the wrist with arm slightly bent.

All measurements in **centimeters**.
