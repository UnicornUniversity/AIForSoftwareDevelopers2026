## 1. Purpose and Scope

Defines the service policies the fictional UNIQLO CZ retailer applies to orders, returns, exchanges, repairs, and customer support. The AI Shopping Consultant may cite this document when a customer asks about returns, delivery, repairs, or complaints. For product-level data (colors, sizes, prices, materials), the consultant must defer to the catalog, data sheets, and price lists.

Applies to purchases from:

- UNIQLO CZ physical stores (Prague, Brno, Ostrava, Pilsen — four fictional stores for exercise purposes).
- UNIQLO CZ online store (fictional).

## 2. Order Confirmation and Delivery

### 2.1 Order confirmation
Online orders receive an email confirmation at the registered address at the moment the order is placed. The confirmation contains the order number, itemized SKUs, order total in CZK (VAT 21% included per `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §7), and the delivery address.

### 2.2 Delivery options

| Option | Standard window | Fee (full-price order) | Fee if order ≥ 1 500 Kč |
|--------|-----------------|------------------------|-------------------------|
| Home delivery (CZ only) | 2–4 working days | 99 Kč | Free |
| Parcel locker (Zásilkovna-equivalent partner) | 2–3 working days | 69 Kč | Free |
| Click & Collect (any CZ store) | 1–2 working days | Free | Free |

Delivery windows are indicative. Peak periods (end-of-season markdowns, December) may extend the window by up to two working days — this is communicated by email the day after dispatch.

### 2.3 Delivery exclusions
UNIQLO CZ delivers only within Czechia. Cross-border orders are not supported in the SS26/AW26 window.

## 3. Returns

### 3.1 Return window
Full-price items may be returned within **30 calendar days** of delivery (online) or of purchase (in store). The window applies regardless of reason.

### 3.2 Condition
Items must be returned:

- unworn and unwashed,
- with original tags attached,
- in resalable condition,
- with proof of purchase (order number for online; receipt or digital receipt for in-store).

Items that fail any of these conditions are returned to the customer at no cost; the sale is not refunded.

### 3.3 Return channels

| Channel | How |
|---------|-----|
| Any UNIQLO CZ store | Bring the item and proof of purchase to the service desk. Refund issued to the original payment method. |
| Online return label | Request label via the online account; drop at the parcel locker. Return shipping is free. |

### 3.4 Refund timing
Refunds are initiated within **3 working days** of the return being accepted. The amount appears on the original payment method within an additional 2–5 working days depending on the issuer.

### 3.5 Non-returnable items
The following are non-returnable except under §4 (defects):

- Underwear worn directly against the skin (not currently in the SS26/AW26 catalog).
- Opened sealed hygiene items.

All items currently listed in SS26 and AW26 (see `UQ-SS26-PRL-v1.0.md` and `UQ-AW26-PRL-v1.0.md`) are returnable.

### 3.6 Markdown items
End-of-season markdown items (SS26 after 2026-08-01; AW26 after 2027-02-01) follow the same 30-day window but refunds are issued at the markdown price paid, not the full price.

## 4. Defects and Faulty Goods

### 4.1 Reporting a defect
Manufacturing defects (seam failure, dye bleed outside the first-wash tolerance in `UQ-CAR-v1.0.md` §3.1, broken trim, incorrect labeling) can be reported within **24 months** of the purchase date.

### 4.2 Resolution
UNIQLO CZ offers, at the customer's choice:

- repair at no cost where feasible (see §6);
- replacement with the same SKU where stock allows (status "Available" per `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §9);
- refund if neither repair nor replacement is feasible.

### 4.3 Not treated as defects
The following are documented product characteristics, not defects:

- Natural creasing on linen pieces (`UQ-CAR-v1.0.md` §3.2).
- Pilling on cashmere during the first wears (`UQ-CAR-v1.0.md` §3.5).
- Shrinkage of up to 3% on cotton after the first wash (`UQ-CAR-v1.0.md` §3.1).

Customer-service agents must cite these clauses before declining a claim.

## 5. Exchanges

### 5.1 Same-SKU exchange (size or color)
In-store exchanges to a different size or color of the same product (same `UQ-{SEASON}-{CAT}-{SEQ}`) are processed on the spot where stock exists. Price difference, if any, is settled at the register. No restocking fee.

### 5.2 Cross-SKU exchange
To exchange for a different product, the customer must process a return (§3) and place a new order or purchase. Return and purchase are independent transactions.

### 5.3 Online exchange
Online orders cannot be directly exchanged. Customers return the unwanted item (§3) and place a new order.

---

## 6. Repair and Alteration Service

### 6.1 Free repair window
Minor repairs — missing or loose buttons, hem repairs, small seam repairs — are offered **free of charge within 60 days of purchase** at any UNIQLO CZ store (reiterated from `UQ-CAR-v1.0.md` §6).

### 6.2 Paid alterations
Trouser hemming for jeans and dress pants is available at a nominal fee at all stores. The fee is quoted at the service desk and is independent of the 60-day free repair window.

### 6.3 Exclusions
Repairs are not offered for:

- items purchased more than 60 days earlier (not under warranty per §4);
- modifications beyond the catalog garment specification (adding pockets, changing a silhouette);
- customer-caused damage (burns, cuts, dye marks from a different garment).

Dry-clean-only garments (`UQ-AW26-CJ-004`, `UQ-AW26-CJ-005`, `UQ-AW26-TR-012`) must be clean before being presented for repair.

## 7. Fitting and Try-On

Fitting rooms are available in all UNIQLO CZ stores (reiterated from `UQ-SIZ-MEN-v1.0.md` §8 and `UQ-SIZ-WOM-v1.0.md` §8). Online orders may be returned under §3 if sizing does not match.

The size charts (`UQ-SIZ-MEN-v1.0.md`, `UQ-SIZ-WOM-v1.0.md`) are the authoritative measurement reference. Where a catalog fit note contradicts a chart, the fit note is applied (chart values describe body measurements; fit notes describe how the specific garment is cut on top of those measurements).

## 8. Payment Methods

### 8.1 In store
Cash in CZK, debit or credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and major contactless wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Foreign currencies are not accepted.

### 8.2 Online
Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Czech bank transfer, and major BNPL partners. Cash on delivery is not available.

### 8.3 Currency
All prices and payments are in CZK. Foreign-currency equivalents are not published (`UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §7).

## 9. Pricing Policy

- Prices listed in the price lists (`UQ-SS26-PRL-v1.0.md`, `UQ-AW26-PRL-v1.0.md`) are full prices and include **Czech standard VAT 21%**.
- Markdowns follow the schedule in `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §7 (−30% on remaining stock from 2026-08-01 for SS26 and 2027-02-01 for AW26). Markdowns are applied automatically at the register and in the online basket.
- Price discrepancies between the catalog and the register are resolved in favor of the lower price when a customer flags them before paying. After payment, §4 applies if the discrepancy stems from a labeling defect.
- UNIQLO CZ does not run a loyalty programme or publish percentage-off coupons in the SS26/AW26 window.

## 10. Stock and Availability

### 10.1 Status definitions
Each product has exactly one status per season from the controlled list in `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §9: Available, Online Only, In-Store Only, Pre-Order, End of Season. All SS26 and AW26 products are currently **Available** (per the price lists).

### 10.2 Stock quantities
Specific stock quantities are not published and must not be invented. If a customer asks whether a specific size or color is in stock at a specific store, the AI consultant must state that stock-level data is not part of this corpus and point the customer to a store or the online product page.

### 10.3 Pre-Order
No pre-order items are currently in the SS26 or AW26 catalog. The pre-order status exists in the glossary for future seasons.

## 11. Privacy and Account

- Customer accounts store the email address, delivery addresses, order history, and (optionally) saved sizes.
- Payment card details are tokenized by the payment partner and are not stored by UNIQLO CZ.
- Marketing emails are opt-in only. Opting out does not affect order-related transactional emails (confirmations, dispatch, returns).
- Customers may request export or deletion of their account data via `privacy@uniqlo.example` (fictional address — do not use outside this exercise).

## 12. Complaints and Escalation

### 12.1 First-line channel
Customers may raise questions or complaints via:

- in-store service desk;
- email: `support@uniqlo.example` (fictional);
- phone during store hours: `+420 000 000 000` (fictional).

Target response time for email: **2 working days**. In-store concerns are addressed on the spot where possible.

### 12.2 Escalation
If the first-line response does not resolve the issue, the customer may escalate to the customer-care manager via `care-manager@uniqlo.example` (fictional). Target response time: **5 working days**.

### 12.3 Independent dispute resolution
Under Czech law, consumers have the right to request independent dispute resolution via the Czech Trade Inspection Authority. UNIQLO CZ does not represent this policy as a substitute for statutory consumer rights.

## 13. Interaction Rules for the AI Shopping Consultant

The AI consultant should apply the following rules when a customer asks a policy question:

1. **Cite this document.** Reference `UQ-CSP-v1.0.md` and the relevant section number.
2. **Do not invent timeframes, fees, or exceptions.** If the customer's question is not addressed here, state that and offer to connect the customer to the service desk.
3. **Do not promise stock.** Status "Available" (`UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §9) does not guarantee a specific size at a specific store.
4. **Do not quote discounts beyond the markdown schedule.** The −30% end-of-season markdown is the only recurring discount in SS26/AW26.
5. **Separate policy from product.** When a customer bundles a policy question with a product request, answer the product request from the catalog and the policy question from this document.
6. **Do not claim statutory rights.** Section 12.3 is a pointer only; the consultant must not advise on statutory consumer-rights disputes.

## 14. Change Control

Policy changes require a minor version bump. The following are considered policy-level changes:

- Modified return window, repair window, or warranty window.
- New payment method or new delivery option.
- Changes to the markdown schedule.
- New fictional store, email, or phone contact.

Product-level changes (name, SKU, price, color, size, material) do **not** touch this document; they are governed by `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §13.
