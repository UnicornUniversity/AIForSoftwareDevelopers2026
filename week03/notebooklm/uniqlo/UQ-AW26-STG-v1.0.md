## 1. Collection Direction

"Winter Warm Core" is a layering collection anchored by the **ThermaCore** base-layer line. Three mid-weight knitwear options (merino crewneck, cashmere V-neck, chunky cardigan) feed into four outerwear choices (ultra-light down, down parka, hybrid puffer, wool coats). The palette is autumnal-cool: charcoal, navy, burgundy, beige, off-white; olive and brown provide earth-tone depth.

Three pillars for the season:

- **Autumn Transition** (+15 to +5 °C) — single-layer mid-pieces, light outerwear.
- **Winter Core** (+5 to −5 °C) — base + mid + outerwear.
- **Deep Winter** (−5 to −15 °C) — ThermaCore Extra Warm + knit + heavy outerwear + accessories.

## 2. ThermaCore Layering Guide

The ThermaCore line is designed as a modular system. Pick exactly one base layer per outfit; the rest of the layers scale with temperature.

| Temperature | Base layer | Mid layer | Outer layer |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| +15 to +10 °C | None | UQ-AW26-TS-010 ThermaCore Crew Long Sleeve or Premium Cotton Mock Neck | UQ-AW26-LJ-010 Fleece Full-Zip Jacket |
| +10 to +5 °C | UQ-AW26-TH-001 ThermaCore Crew Top | Fine Knit Turtleneck or Merino Blend Crewneck | UQ-AW26-CJ-001 Ultra Light Down Jacket |
| +5 to −5 °C | UQ-AW26-TH-001 ThermaCore Crew Top | Merino Blend Crewneck or Chunky Cable Cardigan | Seamless Down Parka / Hybrid Down Puffer / Wool Blend Coat |
| −5 to −15 °C | UQ-AW26-TH-002 ThermaCore Extra Warm Top + Leggings/Tights | Cashmere V-Neck Sweater or Chunky Cable Cardigan | Seamless Down Parka / Hybrid Down Puffer |

Add accessories (`UQ-AW26-AC-010`, `UQ-AW26-AC-011`, `UQ-AW26-AC-012`, `UQ-AW26-AC-013`) below −5 °C and whenever wind is a factor.

## 7. Color Pairing Guidance

- **Charcoal + Off-White + Burgundy** — the signature AW26 trio for business and dressy occasions.
- **Navy + Beige + Olive** — soft neutrals for casual rotation.
- **Brown + Beige** — earthy, autumn-transition pairing (corduroy forward).
- **Pink** — single-piece accent only (Fleece-Lined Earmuffs or Fine Knit Turtleneck); keep remainder of the outfit neutral.
- **Yellow (Ultra Light Down Jacket)** — bright outer layer; pair with charcoal, navy, or black bottoms only.

---

## 8. Care Reminders for the Season

- **ThermaCore** garments must not be washed with fabric softener (reduces moisture-wicking).
- **Cashmere** should be hand washed cold and dried flat. Store folded.
- **Wool blend coats** (`UQ-AW26-CJ-004`, `UQ-AW26-CJ-005`, `UQ-AW26-TR-012`) are **DRY_CLEAN_ONLY**.
- **Down outerwear** can be machine washed on gentle at 30 °C — dry with tennis balls to restore loft.
