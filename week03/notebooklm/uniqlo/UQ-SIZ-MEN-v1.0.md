## 1. How to Measure

Measurements below are body measurements, not garment measurements. Measure directly on the body, wearing light clothing.

- **Chest:** around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms, tape horizontal.
- **Waist:** around the natural waistline, at the narrowest point.
- **Hip:** around the fullest part of the hips, approximately 20 cm below the waist.
- **Inseam:** inner leg, from crotch to ankle bone.
- **Neck:** around the base of the neck.
- **Sleeve length:** from the center back of the neck, over the shoulder, down to the wrist with arm slightly bent.

All measurements in **centimeters**.
