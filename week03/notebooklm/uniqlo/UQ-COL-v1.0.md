## 1. Purpose

Defines every color code used across the UNIQLO CZ catalog, pairs each code with a plain-language description, and lists every product offered in that color. The guide complements the controlled color list in `UQ-GLO-v1.0.md` §6 and the color-pairing guidance in `UQ-SS26-STG-v1.0.md` §6 and `UQ-AW26-STG-v1.0.md` §7.

All color codes are 3-letter uppercase tokens and must be used verbatim in SKUs (`UQ-{SEASON}-{CAT}-{SEQ}-{COLOR}-{SIZE}`).

## 2. Color Palette Overview

The SS26 and AW26 collections share a **16-code controlled vocabulary**. Thirteen codes are used in current products; three (KHA, ORG, GRN) are reserved for future seasons and must not be assigned to SS26 or AW26 variants.

## 7. Color Pairing — At a Glance

Full outfit-level pairing guidance lives in `UQ-SS26-STG-v1.0.md` §6 and `UQ-AW26-STG-v1.0.md` §7. The condensed rules below summarize both.

- **Neutrals ↔ neutrals.** Any two of {WHT, OFW, BLK, NVY, GRY, CHA, BEI} coordinate without rule exceptions.
- **Earth tones.** OLI and BRN pair with BEI and OFW; use with NVY or CHA for contrast.
- **BRG as warm accent.** Pair with CHA, OFW, BEI, or NVY. One BRG piece per outfit.
- **BLU, PNK as cool accents.** Keep on tops. Pair with neutral bottoms.
- **YLW as a single-piece pop.** Allowed on outer layer or cap only; never on bottoms. Pair with CHA, NVY, or BLK bottoms.
- **One accent at a time.** Do not combine YLW with BRG, BLU, or PNK in the same outfit.

## 8. Audit and Change Control

- Reserved codes (KHA, ORG, GRN) remain locked for SS26 and AW26. A later season may activate them; doing so requires a minor version bump of this document and an updated product listing.
- When a product gains or loses a color, the change must be reflected in the catalog (`UQ-{SEASON}-CAT`), the data sheet (`UQ-{SEASON}-PDS`), the price list (`UQ-{SEASON}-PRL` — color-neutral but SKU-dependent), the glossary color table if a new code is introduced, and this guide.
- Image placeholders in data sheets name the dominant color of the pictured variant; the data-sheet entry still lists every stocked color.
