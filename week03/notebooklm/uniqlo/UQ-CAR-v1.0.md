## 3. Care Rules by Material Family

### 3.1 Cotton (100% cotton or cotton-blend ≥ 90%)
- Wash: WASH_30, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW.
- Iron: IRON_MED.
- Note: expect up to 3% shrinkage after first wash. Wash similar colors together; dark cottons may bleed during the first two washes.
- Example products: `UQ-SS26-TS-001` Basic Cotton Crew Tee, `UQ-SS26-SH-001` Oxford Short Sleeve Shirt, `UQ-AW26-SH-011` Corduroy Shirt.

### 3.2 Linen and linen blends
- Wash: WASH_30, NO_BLEACH, DRY_FLAT.
- Iron: IRON_MED while slightly damp.
- Note: linen creases naturally; this is a characteristic of the fabric, not a defect.
- Example products: `UQ-SS26-SH-002` Linen Camp Collar Shirt, `UQ-SS26-TR-002` Linen Blend Trousers, `UQ-SS26-DR-001` Linen Shift Dress.

### 3.3 Polyester, nylon, and synthetic blends
- Wash: WASH_30, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW.
- Iron: IRON_LOW. Avoid direct contact for shiny finishes (satin, chiffon) — use a pressing cloth.
- Note: synthetics are low-shrinkage and fast-drying.
- Example products: `UQ-SS26-TS-002` DryFlex Workout Tee, `UQ-SS26-LJ-001` Pocketable Windbreaker, `UQ-SS26-SH-003` Chiffon Blouse.

### 3.4 Wool, merino, and wool blends
- Wash: WASH_30 wool cycle (or hand wash), NO_BLEACH, DRY_FLAT.
- Iron: IRON_LOW with steam.
- Note: do not tumble dry. Reshape while damp. Store folded, not hung.
- Example products: `UQ-AW26-KN-010` Merino Blend Crewneck, `UQ-AW26-KN-012` Chunky Cable Cardigan, `UQ-AW26-TR-012` Wool Blend Dress Pants.

### 3.5 Cashmere
- Wash: hand wash cold with cashmere/wool detergent, NO_BLEACH, DRY_FLAT.
- Iron: IRON_LOW with steam, inside out.
- Note: pilling is normal and reduces after the first few wears. Use a cashmere comb.
- Example products: `UQ-AW26-KN-011` Cashmere V-Neck Sweater, `UQ-AW26-AC-012` Cashmere Blend Scarf.

### 3.6 Down and insulated outerwear
- Wash: WASH_30 gentle cycle, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW with 2–3 tennis balls to redistribute fill.
- Alternative: DRY_CLEAN_ONLY for Wool Blend Coats.
- Iron: do not iron shell fabric.
- Example products: `UQ-AW26-CJ-001` Ultra Light Down Jacket (WASH_30), `UQ-AW26-CJ-004` Wool Blend Chesterfield Coat (DRY_CLEAN_ONLY).

### 3.7 Fleece
- Wash: WASH_30, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW or air dry.
- Iron: do not iron.
- Note: wash inside out to reduce pilling.
- Example products: `UQ-AW26-LJ-010` Fleece Full-Zip Jacket, `UQ-AW26-AC-013` Fleece-Lined Earmuffs.

### 3.8 Viscose, rayon
- Wash: WASH_30 gentle cycle, NO_BLEACH, DRY_FLAT.
- Iron: IRON_LOW inside out.
- Note: viscose weakens when wet; handle gently.
- Example products: `UQ-SS26-TS-003` Breeze Linen Tank, `UQ-SS26-AC-003` Lightweight Scarf.

### 3.9 ThermaCore technical blends
- Wash: WASH_40, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW.
- Iron: not required and not recommended.
- Note: avoid fabric softeners — they reduce moisture-wicking performance.
- Example products: `UQ-AW26-TS-010` ThermaCore Crew Long Sleeve, `UQ-AW26-TH-001` ThermaCore Crew Top, `UQ-AW26-TH-002` ThermaCore Extra Warm Top.

### 3.10 Jeans (denim)
- Wash: WASH_30 inside out, NO_BLEACH, TUMBLE_LOW or air dry.
- Iron: IRON_MED.
- Note: wash dark denim separately for the first three washes. ThermaCore Lined Jeans (`UQ-AW26-JN-010`) should be air dried to protect the lining.

## 4. Stains

For removable stains, pre-treat with mild detergent and cold water before washing. For set stains on wool, cashmere, or dry-clean-only garments, consult a professional cleaner — do not attempt home treatment.

---

## 5. Storage

- **Knitwear and cashmere:** fold and store with cedar or lavender sachets. Avoid hangers (shoulder deformation).
- **Down jackets:** store loose (not vacuum compressed) to preserve loft.
- **Linen and cotton:** fold or hang; both work.
- **Dry-clean-only outerwear:** cover with breathable garment bags between seasons.

---

## 6. Repair and Alterations

Minor repairs (buttons, hems) are offered free of charge within 60 days of purchase. Trouser hemming is available at a nominal fee for jeans and dress pants in all UNIQLO CZ stores.
