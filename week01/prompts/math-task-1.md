Example 1:
Q: A train travels 60 km in 1.5 hours. What's its speed?
A: Speed = distance / time = 60 / 1.5 = 40 km/h.

Example 2:
Q: A cyclist covers 18 km in 45 minutes. What's their speed in km/h?
A: 45 minutes = 0.75 hours. Speed = 18 / 0.75 = 24 km/h.
