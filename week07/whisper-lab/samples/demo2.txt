Prague has grown from a settlement stretching from Prague Castle in the north to the Fort of Vyajarad in the south, to become the capital of a modern European country. With archaeological deposits over 10 m deep, the city serves as a model for the implementation of comprehensive regulations for the protection of archaeological heritage throughout the Czech Republic. The mythological princess Liboe prophesies the glory of Prague. The region was settled as early as the Paleolithic Age.
